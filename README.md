# QA Portfolio: WebAlive Projects

Hi, I'm Anoma. During my QA internship at WebAlive I worked on several modules across our platform: the Operon service management system, the Exsited inventory management app and the Budget and Labour Hire centres. I wrote test cases, ran functional tests, logged bugs and compared our features to competitor products.

This repository contains those reports. If you're reviewing my work, how I approach testing, here's what you'll find inside.

## Repository contents

- **Operon**:
  - `Operon-Evaluation.pdf`: summary of the Operon platform's readiness and enterprise features.
  - `Operon-Functional-Testing-Validation-Report.pdf`: my functional test findings and suggestions for improvement.
- **Exsited**:
  - `Exsited-Feature-Validation-and-Competitor-Comparison.pdf`: validation of Exsited's feature claims with real products and notes on competitor products.
- **Budget module**:
  - `Test-Cases-Budget-Module.pdf`: test scenarios I designed for the Budget module.
  - `Issue-Report-Budget-Module.pdf`: bugs and enhancement suggestions raised during testing.
- **Labour Hire Centre**:
  - `Test-Cases-Labour-Hire-Center.pdf`: test scenarios covering core workflows.
  - `Issue-Report-Labour-Hire-Center.pdf`: defects and usability issues discovered.
- **Rental Asset Centre**:
  - `Test-Cases-Rental-Asset-Center.pdf`: test scenarios covering asset rentals.
- **Issue reporting**:
  - `QA-Issue-Report-Template.pdf`: a reusable issue report template I created for consistent bug logging.
- **Portfolio methodology**:
  - `QA-Portfolio/TESTING-METHODOLOGY.md`: an overview of my testing approach.

## What you'll find

**Operon documents**: insights into the Operon service management platform. The evaluation covers enterprise readiness, workflow maturity and competitor positioning. The functional testing report describes the test cases I executed, the outcomes and my recommendations for improvement.

**Exsited documents**: a feature validation and competitor comparison of Exsited. This report checks the features promised on the marketing site against the live system and highlights areas where Exsited falls short or excels compared to products like StockWise, Cin7 and Unleashed.

**Budget and Labour Hire Centre documents**: detailed test cases covering positive and negative scenarios, along with issue reports describing any bugs or gaps I found. These files demonstrate how I design test scenarios, record results and communicate issues to developers.

Thanks for taking the time to look through my QA portfolio! Feel free to reach out if you have any questions about my testing process or the documents in this repository.
